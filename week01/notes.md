## What I learnt
- Error(F silent) is the difference between target and the output.
- Loss is the function of errors. 0 is the minimum value for loss.
- Training a model is trying to reduce the loss and when it reached to the least loss its called Trained ML model/NN.
- ML subdomains are Supervised learning(Labeled data), Unsupervised learning(unlabeled data), Semi-supervised, Reinforcement.
- Semi-supervised introduced due to lack of posibility to make the large amount of labeled data. For this uses minor amount of labeled data with the massive amount of unlabled data.
- Reinforcement is within a controlled environment rewarding(positive/negative) to the actions and based on the rewards learning to follow certain patterns.
- Data Collection -> Preprocessing -> EDA -> Feature Engineering -> Model Selection -> Training -> Evaluation -> Deployment.
- MSE, MAE and RMSE are loss functions.
